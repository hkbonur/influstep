# Influstep

**Register**: product

## What it is

Influstep runs AI personas that publish short videos on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram so that the people who follow them discover Formstep. One operator sets up a persona; the persona's agents research what the audience talks about, write and record episodes on the operator's computer, publish on schedule, read the comments, and report whether followers became Formstep users. Every persona runs experiments, so the operator learns which concepts earn attention and which earn conversions, and steers with plain-language directives.

The business goal: attention and users for Formstep without buying ads or renting human influencers. Later, the same software sold to other companies that want their own personas.

## Who uses it

One operator today: the Formstep founder, technical, short on time, checking in a few times a week from a laptop. They want to glance at a calendar and a report, approve or redirect, and do the two or three things only a human can (sign in to n8n, grant a YouTube permission). They never want to assemble a video by hand.

## Tone

Calm, plain, honest. The app reports what the personas did and what it cost in attention, in numbers the operator can read in ten seconds. It never hypes a persona's results and never hides a failure: a failed job sits on the calendar in red with the error.

Copy uses the glossary in CONTEXT.md: persona, episode, signal, experiment, checklist item, tracked link, report.

## Strategic principles

1. **One screen answers "is it working?"** The persona report shows followers, views, clicks, signups, and paid as a funnel. Everything else is one click deeper.
2. **The calendar is the home.** The week view shows every persona's plan, what is blocked on the operator, and what failed.
3. **The operator approves, the agents do.** Scripts wait for approval by default; recording, voicing, rendering, publishing, and measuring run on their own.
4. **Experiments are the method, not a feature.** Every episode carries a variant when an experiment is open, and the report shows what won.
5. **Interesting first, sponsor second.** A persona earns followers with unsponsored episodes; the sponsor dial sets how often Formstep appears, and the planner keeps new personas unsponsored at first.
6. **Nothing silent.** Missing keys, failed uploads, and unreachable tools surface as checklist items or failed jobs, never as mock data pretending to be real.

## Anti-references

- Social media management dashboards with twelve widgets and a funnel chart in every color: the report is one funnel and one line chart.
- "Content factories" that spray generic AI videos: a persona has three to five content pillars and a stable voice, and the writer reads the directives.
- Growth hacks that hide the sponsor: every sponsored episode says so, and every publication carries the platform's AI label.

## Surfaces

- **Calendar** (home): week view across personas; cards by episode status; blocked and failed states loud.
- **Persona**: chat with directives, script editor with approval, checklist, pillars, sponsor dial, pause.
- **Report**: per persona funnel and trend; experiments with their decisions.
- **Experiments**: open and decided experiments with per-variant numbers.
- **Signals**: what the researchers found, by source, with "make an episode" one click away.
- **Checklist**: everything waiting on the operator, across personas.
- **Providers**: which adapters run in mock or real mode, and the runner's status.
